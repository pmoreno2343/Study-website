Chest Pain Triage Algorithm

Step-by-step clinical pathway.

1
Assess stability
ABCDE • vitals • monitor
↓
2
Look for immediate killers
ACS • aortic dissection • PE • tension PTX • tamponade • esophageal rupture
↓
3
ECG early
Obtain 12-lead ECG promptly if ACS is possible
↓
4
Focused testing
hs-troponin ± CXR / CTA / echo / PE work-up according to the pattern
↓
5
Reassess
Repeat ECG/troponin if suspicion persists; escalate instability

Acute Coronary Syndrome Algorithm

Step-by-step clinical pathway.

1
Ischemic symptoms
ECG + monitor + IV access + hs-troponin
↓
2
Persistent diagnostic ST elevation?
YES → STEMI reperfusion pathway immediately
↓
3
No persistent ST elevation
Serial hs-troponin + risk assessment → NSTEMI / UA / alternative diagnosis
↓
4
Initial therapy
Aspirin • statin • anti-ischemic therapy when appropriate • antithrombotic therapy per pathway
↓
5
Reperfusion / invasive strategy
Primary PCI preferred when timely; selected early STEMI may need fibrinolysis if PCI delay is excessive

Hypertension Diagnosis Algorithm

Step-by-step clinical pathway.

1
Accurate office BP
Correct cuff • seated rest • repeat measurements
↓
2
Elevated reading
Review prior readings, medications and contributing factors
↓
3
Confirm
Home or ambulatory BP monitoring when feasible
↓
4
Assess risk
ASCVD risk • kidney disease • diabetes • target-organ damage
↓
5
Treat
Lifestyle for all; pharmacotherapy based on BP, global risk and comorbidities

Severe Hypertension Decision Pathway

Step-by-step clinical pathway.

1
Very high BP
Repeat correctly and assess symptoms
↓
2
Acute target-organ damage?
Brain • heart • aorta • kidney • retina
↓
3A
YES → hypertensive emergency
Monitored setting + titratable IV therapy + condition-specific target
↓
3B
NO
Avoid rapid IV normalization; adjust oral therapy and ensure close follow-up
↓
4
Remember special cases
Aortic dissection, stroke and pregnancy use specific targets
